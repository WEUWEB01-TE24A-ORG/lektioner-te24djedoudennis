Costa rica


Sloths live in trees in Central and South America.
They move very slowly and eat mostly leaves. They sleep for many hours a day and are excellent swimmers.
Sloths have long claws and can live up to 20-30 years.

Costa Rica is a country in Central America. Its capital is San José.
The country is famous for its beautiful beaches, rainforests and wildlife.
The official language is Spanish. Costa Rica has many animals, such as monkeys, sloths and toucans.

Toucans live in the tropical rainforests of Central and South America.
They are famous for their large, colorful beaks, which help them reach food. They mainly eat fruit, but also insects, eggs and small animals.
Toucans are excellent climbers and use their feet to grip branches. They usually live in small groups and build their nests in holes in trees.
Their colorful appearance helps them stand out in the rainforest.
